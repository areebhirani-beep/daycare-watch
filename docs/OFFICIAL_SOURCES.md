# Official sources

Every phone number, URL, and legal claim shown in the app must trace to a row here. All rows were checked against the live official page on **Oct 24 2026**. If a row is marked UNVERIFIED, do not ship it.

**Corrections to earlier assumptions:**
- **SB 1098 is from the 88th Legislature (2023), not the 89th (2025).** 89(R) SB 1098 is an unrelated pharmacy drug-pricing bill. Always cite it as **88(R) SB 1098**.
- **SB 1098 does give parents drop-in, records, and video rights.** The video right is narrow, though: parents may *inspect* video of an *alleged abuse/neglect incident*, only *if available*, and only after other parents are notified. It is **not** a right to live or general camera access.
- **DFPS no longer accepts anonymous child abuse reports (since Sep 1 2023).** The CCR online form also requires a name.
- **The state site already offers per-operation email updates** ("Sign Up for or Unsubscribe from Updates" on every OperationDetails page). Our alert feature must not claim to be the only way to get notified.
